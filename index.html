Christopher J. Rothfuss, Ph.D.

Wyoming State Senator · University of Wyoming Honors College · Chemical Engineer

About

I represent Senate District 9 in Laramie in the Wyoming State Senate, where I have served since 2011, including twelve years as Senate Minority Leader. I created and chair the legislature's Select Committee on Blockchain, Financial Technology & Digital Innovation Technology, and I have spent fifteen years on Wyoming's education and minerals committees.

At the University of Wyoming I teach in the Honors College and direct its internships, research and entrepreneurship programs. Before that I was a AAAS Science & Technology Diplomacy Fellow at the U.S. Department of State, an inventor on 16 issued U.S. patents, and an oilfield engineer. My Ph.D. is in chemical engineering from the University of Washington.

I learned BASIC on an Atari in 1983 and have been writing code ever since.
