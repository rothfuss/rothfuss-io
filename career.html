Chairman, Board of Directors | Pilot Hill, Inc., Laramie, WY (5/19–9/20)
Chairman, Oversight Committee | Pilot Hill Project, Laramie, WY (10/17–9/20)

Selected by the Albany County Board of Commissioners to serve as the founding Chairman of the Pilot Hill Project Oversight Committee. The mission of the Pilot Hill Project was to facilitate the public acquisition of nearly 5,500 acres of beautiful, rugged and undeveloped land east of Laramie to provide recreational opportunities, facilitate wildlife management and protect Casper aquifer drinking water.

  • Led the development and implementation of a multi-year community strategy to raise $14.5M to acquire and manage ~5,500 acres of land on behalf of the citizens of Albany County, Wyoming;
  • Organized Finance, Communications and Land Management subcommittees to engage community stakeholders and coordinate hundreds of community volunteers;
  • Successfully secured a land exchange between the Pilot Hill land owners and the State of Wyoming for all land under consideration to place the land in public hands;
  • Oversaw the raising of >$1M of grants and community donations to fund Pilot Hill, Inc. administration, operations and infrastructure;
  • Organized Pilot Hill, Inc. as a non-profit corporation to oversee recreation and land management leases in cooperation with the State of Wyoming, Albany County and the Wyoming Department of Game and Fish;
  • Wyoming Planning Association 2020 Planning Project of the Year.

Candidate for United States Senate | Rothfuss for Senate, Wyoming (3/08–11/08)

In 2008, I was the Democratic candidate in the Wyoming election for the United States Senate. After winning the primary and the Democratic nomination, I was defeated by Republican incumbent Senator Mike Enzi in the general election on November 4, 2008.

My campaign platform emphasized:

  • Achieving sustainable energy independence through a comprehensive long term national energy plan, the establishment of a National Energy Board and an Energy Bond program (Op-Ed: “Bonds, Energy Bonds”), and a sensible Cap and Trade policy (Op-Ed: “Energy leadership, not just energy production”);
  • Reliance on sound science in the legislative process;
  • A return to global diplomatic leadership and the withdrawal of our troops from Iraq; and
  • Regaining the Constitutional rights and freedoms that have been eroded by the Executive Branch.

AAAS Science & Technology Diplomacy Fellow and Foreign Affairs Officer (GS-14) | U.S. Department of State, Office of Space & Advanced Technology, Washington, DC (9/03–8/06)

While working at the Department of State, I was responsible for providing expert scientific advice and analysis to aid in the development of U.S. foreign policy. My primary role was serving as the senior nanotechnology advisor for the State Department and the U.S. government lead on international nanotechnology issues. In this capacity, I represented U.S. interests in multilateral and bilateral meetings and negotiations, and coordinated U.S. foreign policy in cooperation with other federal agencies and the intelligence community on nanotechnology related issues. In addition, my portfolio included advanced technologies, energy, space and advanced technology export control and licensing, orbital debris mitigation, space nuclear power sources, object re-entry, remote sensing and the UN Committee on the Peaceful Uses of Outer Space (COPUOS). I represented my office and the Department on the following committees and working groups:

  • NSTC Nanoscale Science, Engineering and Technology (NSET) Subcommittee
  • Chairman, NSET Global Issues in Nanotechnology (GIN) Working Group
  • Chairman, U.S. Department of State Nanotechnology Working Group
  • NSET Nanotechnology Environmental and Health Implications (NEHI) Working Group
  • Nano-Enabled Technologies Initiative (NETI) Technical Working Group
  • Missile Technology Export Control (MTEC) Committee
  • Remote Sensing Interagency Working Group (RSIWG)
  • NOAA Informal Interagency Remote Sensing Working Group (NIIRSWG)
  • International Inter-Agency Space Debris Coordination Committee (IADC)
  • UN COPUOS Space Debris Working Group
  • UN COPUOS Space Nuclear Power Sources Working Group

Select accomplishments:

  • Negotiated and concluded the UN Space Debris Mitigation Guidelines working within the UN COPUOS Scientific and Technical Subcommittee Space Debris Working Group.
  • Founded the Global Issues in Nanotechnology (GIN) Working Group of the NSTC Nanoscale Science, Engineering, and Technology (NSET) Subcommittee and served as chairman to establish a venue for coordinating U.S. nanotechnology foreign policy.
  • Spearheaded the creation of the Working Party on Nanotechnology within the Committee for Scientific and Technological Policy (CSTP) of the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD) to coordinate and facilitate the cooperative global development of nanotechnology.

Doctoral Research | Chemical Engineering Dept., University of Washington, Seattle, WA (9/98–12/02)

While working on my PhD research, I worked at the University of Washington Electrochemical Surface Science (UWESS) group under Prof. Eric Stuve. My research focused on high field surface chemistry - primarily using ultra high vacuum surface science techniques, such as field ion microscopy, field emission microscopy, field desorption microscopy, pulsed time of flight mass spectrometry, and ExB ion mass spectrometry.

Consulting Engineer | Chemical Tracers, Inc., Laramie, WY (9/97–11/09)

Chemical Tracers, Inc. (CTI) is an oilfield service company that specializes in in situ fluid saturation measurements, including residual oil saturation, connate water saturation and other key reservoir parameters. Measurements are accomplished using reactive tracers in a Single Well Chemical Tracer (SWCT) test program. Services are primarily utilized for the assessment of Enhanced Oil Recovery (EOR) program efficacy, or for assessing oil in place. For over a decade, I worked for CTI as a field engineer, crew chief, director of engineering, and then on an ongoing consulting basis. I have performed numerous residual oil tests in fields throughout the world, including the US (Alaska, California, New Mexico), Brazil, Russia and Venezuela; carried out EOR one spot pilot tests to evaluate the efficacy of alkaline / surfactant / polymer and CO2 flooding test programs; continued the development of the computer simulations that model in situ chemical tracer reactivity and fluid flow; prepared and secured contract proposals including CTI's first $1M+ contract; and worked with CTI's President to develop business strategy and manage business operations.

Research Engineer | CHA Corporation, Laramie, WY (4/97–9/97)

As a Research Engineer at CHA Corporation, my duties included experimental design, data analysis, and the preparation of reports and proposals. CHA Corporation is a research and development firm focusing on microwave induced chemical reactions for environmental remediation. Areas of research included Carbon Adsorbent Systems, Diesel Exhaust Treatment, Microwave Reactor Techniques and NOx / SOx / Volatile Organic Compound Cleanup.

Computer Systems Administrator | National Centre for Petroleum Geology and Geophysics, Adelaide, South Australia (10/96–2/97)

As Computer Systems Administrator, I assessed the initial state of computer operations at the facility, analyzed the hardware and software trends of industry in Australia and recommended a course of action for upgrading the existing inventory. In addition, I supervised the purchase and installation of the suggested network and workstations, and served as administrator for my remaining time in Australia.
